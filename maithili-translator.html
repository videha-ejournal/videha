In Maithili the word-final क and त are written in formal dictionaries but the word is equally correct without them. Both spellings now resolve to the same entry — e.g. जाइ ≡ जाइत (go), छलै ≡ छलैक (was). The table marks such matches as “variant (±क/त)”.
